# T1430.001: Remote Device Management Services

**Matrix:** Mobile | **Type:** Sub-technique

## Description
An adversary may use access to cloud services (e.g. Google's Android Device Manager or Apple iCloud's Find my iPhone) or to an enterprise mobility management (EMM)/mobile device management (MDM) server console to track the location of mobile devices managed by the service.(Citation: Krebs-Location)

## Tactics
Collection, Discovery

## Platforms
Android, iOS

## Procedure
An adversary may use access to cloud services (e.g. Google's Android Device Manager or Apple iCloud's Find my iPhone) or to an enterprise mobility management (EMM)/mobile device management (MDM) server console to track the location of mobile devices managed by the service.(Citation: Krebs-Location)

## Mitigations
- **Enterprise Policy** — An enterprise mobility management (EMM), also known as mobile device management (MDM), system can be used to provision policies to mobile devices to control aspects of their allowed behavior.
- **User Guidance** — Describes any guidance or training given to users to set particular configuration settings or avoid specific potentially risky behaviors.
